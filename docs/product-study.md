# Test whether people return to BadShop

The product hypothesis is that small creator groups will reuse their own characters
and remix each other's jokes. Shipping features does not establish market fit. This
four-week study measures actual creation, sharing, reuse and voluntary return.

## Recruit and ask permission

The maintainer can recruit 15–20 adults across group-chat creators, meme communities
and people who already share recurring character jokes. Include Windows, macOS and
Linux users; record the exact released build. Recruitment and outreach have not been
performed by adding this kit. Do not send invitations without the maintainer's approval.

Explain the purpose, tasks, time commitment, any compensation and retention policy.
Participation and sharing examples are optional. Let people withdraw without penalty.
Use participant codes. Do not collect names, private group-chat contents or third-party
faces unless separately consented. Agree a deletion date (for example 30 days after
the report). Store records with restricted access, not in this public repository.

## Sessions and diary

Week 1: watch each participant install, choose their own picture, finish a joke and
save or copy it. Start the timer at installation completion. Do not explain controls
unless they ask; record help separately. Ask what they would otherwise have used.

Weeks 2–3: offer optional access to cast, recipes, remix sharing and challenges. Avoid
daily reminders that manufacture retention. Ask participants to keep this small diary
only when they choose to use the app:

| Date and participant code | Trigger for using it | Finished images | Actually shared? | Reused character/recipe? | Received/opened a remix? | Blocker or outside tool needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | |

Week 4: ask for a short interview. What brought you back? What did someone else do
with the result? Which feature did you ignore? What would you use if BadShop disappeared?
Would you independently recommend it to a friend? Request an optional non-private
example; do not treat expressed enthusiasm as observed return.

## Decide before seeing the results

These are proposed decision gates, not industry benchmarks or measured outcomes:

- Activation: at least 70% finish their own joke within five minutes of opening the
  installed app, without facilitator help. Also report median time and install failures.
- Utility: at least 50% actually share a result outside the study during week 1.
- Return: at least 40% of activated participants return voluntarily in week 2, and
  at least 30% in week 4. Report both activated and all-recruited denominators.
- Reuse: at least 30% of activated participants reuse a character, recipe or someone
  else's editable remix on a different day.
- Community: at least three independent people submit a usable pack, recipe or
  actionable contribution without the facilitator writing it for them.

Report counts as well as percentages, nonresponses, incentives, assistance and sampling
limitations. A small convenience sample cannot prove broad market fit. Stars and download
counts are distribution signals; they are not retention.

If activation fails, fix installation and first-result friction before adding more formats.
If sharing works but return fails, interview around the real next occasion to make a joke.
If cast reuse works but remix exchange fails, improve reusable characters before building
online collaboration. Preserve negative findings alongside positive ones.

## Launch material to use after release verification

Suggested description: “Make terrible pictures with people you know. BadShop is a free,
open-source editor with instant joke formats, reusable characters, editable remix files
and creation GIFs. Start without an AI account; keep every edit replayable.”

Capture a 30-second demonstration from the verified release: import a picture → compare
ideas → make a joke → change a punchline → export a remix → reopen and change it. Link
the [download guide](downloads.md), [tutorial](playful-workflows.md) and
[contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Publish only after the release includes the
demonstrated features. Do not claim study results, signing or platform verification
that has not occurred.
